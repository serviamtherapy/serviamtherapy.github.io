Accessibility Statement

Last updated: September 14, 2026

1. Our Commitment

Serviam Therapy is committed to making its website accessible and usable for people with disabilities. We aim to provide an inclusive digital experience and to improve accessibility as the website develops.

2. Accessibility Approach

We work to follow recognized accessibility principles, including clear structure, readable content, keyboard access, meaningful link text, sufficient visual contrast, and compatibility with commonly used assistive technologies.

The website is developed with consideration of applicable accessibility requirements in Israel, including the principles of Israeli accessibility regulations and the Israeli Standard SI 5568, as applicable to the website.

3. Accessibility Features

  • Semantic headings and structured page content where practical.
  • Keyboard-focus indicators for interactive elements.
  • Alternative text for meaningful images where practical.
  • Responsive layouts designed for different screen sizes.
  • Support for reduced-motion preferences for certain animated elements.
  • Forms and interactive controls designed to be understandable and usable.

4. Known Limitations

Accessibility can vary depending on the device, browser, operating system, assistive technology, third-party services, and external content. Some third-party components or older content may not fully meet every accessibility requirement.

We continuously review the website and welcome reports of barriers so that we can address issues where reasonably possible.

5. Feedback and Assistance

If you experience difficulty using any part of the website, please contact Serviam Therapy through the communication channels published on the website. Please describe the page or feature involved and, if possible, the type of difficulty you encountered.

6. Accessibility Coordinator

For accessibility-related questions or requests, please use the contact information published on the website. If a dedicated accessibility coordinator is appointed, their details should be added to this section.

7. Ongoing Improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing process. We may update the website, its code, content, and third-party integrations to improve accessibility and usability.

Note: This statement describes the site's accessibility commitment. It should be reviewed and finalized with the actual accessibility implementation and, where required, with professional legal/accessibility advice for the specific business.
